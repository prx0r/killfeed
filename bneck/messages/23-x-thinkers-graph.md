# The actual worldview emerging

Combining these thinkers gives something much more interesting than “AGI makes Nvidia go up.”

### I. Intelligence ceases being the scarce input

Models increasingly turn cognition into a utility. Emad explicitly thinks in these terms; Altman has similarly described intelligence increasingly like metered infrastructure. ([ii][29])

### II. Intelligence starts optimizing its own substrate

Normal/Extropic/EDA/photonic/neuromorphic systems create:

**AI → hardware search → novel hardware → cheaper AI → larger hardware search.**

This is the first major recursive acceleration loop.

### III. Models acquire simulated worlds

World models make enormous quantities of interactive experience synthetically available.

### IV. Reality becomes the training bottleneck

Simulation eventually needs grounding.

Robots need objects.

Scientists need experimental results.

Biologists need cells.

Chip designers need tape-outs.

So **privileged interaction with reality appreciates relative to pure cognition**.

### V. Science becomes closed-loop

Agents don't just propose discoveries. They control laboratories.

And then **experiment throughput becomes compute-like infrastructure**.

### VI. Biology itself becomes programmable

This is where Levin + Cronin + synthetic cells + Cortical Labs become profound.

Cells already possess:

* sensing,
* actuation,
* memory,
* repair,
* replication,
* distributed control,
* learning/adaptation,
* extremely efficient computation,
* nanoscale manufacturing.

We may discover that trying to reproduce every desirable property of life using lithography was the wrong abstraction.

### VII. “Computer” becomes substrate-independent

Digital CMOS is one implementation.

Photonics, stochastic electronics, thermodynamics, living neurons, biochemical networks and other physical dynamical systems become alternative ways of implementing transformations.

That's **the really big Normal/Levin intersection**.

### VIII. Scarcity doesn't disappear

It shifts into:

**energy → atoms → experiments → fabrication → bandwidth → embodiment → unique data → permissions → ownership → physical locations → time.**

And then AGI attacks each of those in turn.

The interesting investments are where markets incorrectly assume a bottleneck will remain—or incorrectly assume one will disappear.
