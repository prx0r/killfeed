# Papers: AI obsolescence / step-change creative destruction (2026-09-10)

Thesis: AI obsoletes industries via step-function capability/cost shocks.
Verdict from literature: CONFIRMED at labor/task level, with precise structure.
Implications for our engine at the end.

## Firm value & exposure

1. **Eisfeldt–Schubert–Zhang, NBER w31222 (2023): Generative AI and Firm Values.**
   First firm-level GenAI workforce-exposure measure. AMH (long high-exposure,
   short low) earned ~5% in 2 weeks post-ChatGPT (0.44–0.45%/day, t=3.53).
   Stronger with data assets; distinct from product exposure. Mechanism =
   SUBSTITUTION channel (job posts, wages, profitability). Core-task exposure
   effects 2x average; CAR impact +75% for core-task exposure. No pre-trend,
   no reversal; second run-up into GPT-4.
2. **Eisfeldt–Schubert, NBER w33076 (2024): AI and Finance (review).**
   ChatGPT = large tech shock, generally positive but cross-sectionally wide.
   Italy ChatGPT ban: exposed Italian firms fell (small/young worst).
   Open direction: corporate policies (capex, capital structure) post-shock.
3. **Borri–Tsyvinski–Liu, NBER w35451 (2026): AI Premium.**
   380T tokens OpenRouter (~2% global monthly). AI factor + firm AI betas:
   value-weighted long-short **64.1bp/week**, loadings on frontier/intensive
   margin (closed models, paying users, long prompts). Beyond tech into
   consumer/capital-heavy; absent in EM. Interaction-communication occupations
   +0.36sd exposure; analytical/scientific/operations negative. Early agentic
   economy evidence. → DIRECTLY TESTABLE HERE (E055).
4. **Ma, NBER w29504 (2021): Technological Obsolescence.**
   Patent-based obsolescence: high-obsolescence firms underperform **7%/yr**;
   analysts SYSTEMATICALLY OVERESTIMATE their profits. → Edge is long-horizon
   short vs street estimates, not event-day shorting.

## Labor / freelance (causal DiD)

5. **Hui–Reshef–Zhou (Upwork, 92K freelancers): writing -2% jobs/-5.2% pay;**
   extensive margin -1.2pp (~-10%); top freelancers hit HARDER; DALL-E/Midjourney
   same pattern for design.
6. **Qiao–Rui–Qian: inflection-point model.** Translation -9% vol/-30% earnings
   (displacement); webdev +6.4%/+66% (productivity). Honeymoon phase (AI helps)
   → substitution phase (AI hurts) per occupation; GPT-3.5→4 flipped some
   occupations. Affected: writing/translation/support/accounting/legal/compliance;
   safe-ish: complex programming/creative. Experienced translators exit first.
7. **JEBO 235 (2025): ~10% of postings substitutable; demand -20–50%**
   substitutable short-term roles; ML +24%, chatbot dev 3x; novice demand down
   even in complementary clusters (expertise bar rises).
8. **Demirci–Hannane–Zhu: -21% automation-prone posts;** writing -30%, SWE -21%,
   eng -10%; image -17%. Google-Trends awareness correlates with declines.
9. **Siddiq–Zhang (2026, 49K workers/2.26M contracts to 2026Q1): COMMODITIZATION.**
   Human-capital signal importance -7.8% (-10.1% by 2026Q1), price +1.1%;
   premium for credentials falls; demand shifts to cheaper workers. Effects
   STILL WIDENING 3+ years post-shock — no re-equilibration.
10. **Serbia chatbots (2025): -17.8% customer-service FTEs;** retail worst,
    restructuring not layoffs; hybrid models.
11. **IMF WP/25/43 Philippines: BPO = high exposure/low complementarity;**
    14% of workforce displaceable; BPO 7.4% of GDP (macro-critical);
    8% of AI-using firms cut staff, 13% added new AI roles.

## Productivity side (why incumbents survive)

12. **Brynjolfsson–Li–Raymond: +15% issues/hour (5,172 agents);** novices +30–36%,
    top agents flat/down in quality. AI compresses skill gaps (commoditization
    mechanism at firm level).
13. **Alibaba field exp (2026, 5,940 agents): speed + quality up;** top agents'
    quality DOWN via multitasking. Deployment strategy matters.

## Case study

14. **Chegg: WSJ "first major victim" (-99%, -500K subs, -40% workforce,**
    revenue down every year since 2021, now suing Google over AI Overviews).
    Mechanism: answers → zero marginal cost; leadership dismissed threat
    (accuracy), internal tests showed GPT-4 beating human experts; Cheggmate
    stillborn. Textbook labor-arbitrage death.
