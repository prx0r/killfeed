# AGI Bottleneck Intelligence Resource Pack
Generated: 2026-09-10

## Thesis

**AGI changes the economic dependency graph.** A capability breakthrough can make a previously scarce input more valuable, or suddenly make that input unnecessary. The system should estimate:

1. what is binding now;
2. what becomes binding if the current constraint is relieved;
3. what new capability could destroy each scarcity rent;
4. when the probability of that destruction changes enough to matter;
5. which securities have the cleanest exposure to the repricing.

The highest-convexity short setup is a richly valued/pure company whose terminal value assumes a bottleneck persists while hard capability evidence indicates that dependency may disappear.

## Frontier labs as informed actors

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta and xAI have unusually good private information about frontier-model limitations, infrastructure pain, scaling laws, model/system co-design and what they are willing to reserve/build/buy years in advance. Their capital allocation is therefore a **revealed-preference stream**.

Do not blindly copy it. A transaction may be a hedge, supplier diversification, bargaining leverage, acqui-hire or supply insurance. Weight commitments by **irreversibility, specificity, size, frontier relevance and lead time**.

Especially strong observations:
- custom silicon/tape-outs and multi-generation hardware partnerships;
- multi-year compute reservations;
- GW-scale PPAs/data-center/site commitments;
- strategic supplier equity alignment;
- narrow infrastructure acquisitions;
- major hiring clusters in a new technical domain.

## Proof before narrative

Normal Computing and Extropic are exactly the right type of thing to watch: potential **diggers** that could alter accelerator/energy/memory dependencies. Company claims are not incumbent-kill triggers. Tier A requires economically relevant, repeatable evidence plus a credible deployment and scale path.

## The signal stack

**Fast / noisy:** equity/options, Polymarket/Kalshi, X/expert streams.

**Implementation / technical:** GitHub, Hugging Face, benchmarks, arXiv/OpenAlex/preprints.

**Hard / slow(er):** patents, SEC/shareholder filings, purchase commitments, government awards/procurement, export-control/legal documents, trade/capacity/power/minerals data.

The edge often comes from **disagreement between clocks**.

## Executives' money

For public-company executives, Form 4 is the cleanest U.S. source for insider transactions in their public company's shares. 13D/G and 13F reveal other holdings only when filing thresholds/entity requirements apply. There is no comprehensive free mandatory database of every personal investment made by executives of private frontier labs. Use Form D, applicable 13D/G/13F filings, official company/fund announcements, disclosed strategic investments, supplier filings and acquisition disclosures. Treat press-reconstructed personal portfolios as incomplete.

The stronger signal is usually **the frontier lab's own irreversible corporate allocation** rather than trying to reconstruct a CEO's private brokerage account.

## Recommended MVP

Start with `DuckDB + Parquet + NetworkX + normalized signals + information lineage`. Make these feeds autonomous first:

1. frontier-lab acquisitions/capacity/custom-silicon/power;
2. Polymarket/Kalshi;
3. GitHub/Hugging Face;
4. SEC/Form 4/13F/8-K;
5. OpenAlex/patents/grants;
6. physical spot/capacity/inventory data for live bottlenecks.

Then run `CONSTRAIN()` and `DESTROY()` recursively over the graph and backtest every state transition against historical bottleneck corpses.

## Critical caveat

A 70% efficiency gain is not automatically a short. Apply a Jevons/demand-elasticity correction before declaring a dependency dead.
