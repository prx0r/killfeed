# Executive and founder capital OSINT

## Public-company executives
Strongest structured sources:
- SEC Form 4: insider buys/sells/options in their own public company
- Form 3/Form 5: ownership entry and annual corrections/reporting
- Schedule 13D/13G: large beneficial ownership stakes when thresholds apply
- 13F: institutional investment managers, not ordinary individuals
- proxy statements (DEF 14A): compensation, ownership and related-party disclosures

## Private frontier-lab executives
There is no complete mandatory public feed of personal investments. Build an incomplete evidence graph from:
- Form D filed by portfolio companies raising private capital
- official angel/fund portfolio pages
- company acquisition/investment announcements
- public interviews/podcasts only when the transaction is concrete
- state/company registries where relevant
- 13D/G when a public-equity stake becomes reportable

Never turn absence of disclosure into evidence of absence.

## Better signal than private portfolios
The frontier lab's **corporate** commitments are usually cleaner:
- custom silicon
- multi-year compute contracts
- power deals
- acquisitions
- strategic suppliers
- patents/IP assignments
- hiring and facilities

These are larger, closer to the model-development bottleneck and easier to verify.
