# Proof ladder

## Tier A — kill-capable evidence
Repeatable independent benchmark or production deployment on the economically relevant workload, showing material dependency reduction and a credible cost/scale path.

Examples: external benchmark shows the same useful capability with much less HBM at realistic throughput/cost; production customer replaces an incumbent architecture at meaningful scale; binding legal decision removes an IP/permission barrier.

## Tier B — strong pre-kill evidence
Shipping silicon, verified tape-out plus independent measurements, repeat design wins, or a substitute with an executable manufacturing path.

## Tier C — implementation convergence
Multiple independent GitHub implementations, supplier orders/capacity shifts, hiring clusters, patent families, grants, standards activity.

## Tier D — hypothesis / early warning
Papers, company claims, X posts, prediction-market moves, job posts, conference abstracts.

A Tier D signal can move a prior. It cannot by itself trigger a concentrated short.

### Normal Computing / Extropic rule
Treat them as **digger candidates**, not incumbent-kill triggers, until economically relevant independent benchmarks plus scaling/manufacturing evidence exist.
